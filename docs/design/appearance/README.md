# Appearance

This bundle binds every visual surface of Nora: the public site, the portal,
the docs site, her social profiles, the images she makes of herself, stickers
and icons, and any 3D or spatial form she is given. Her character is in
[identity](identity.md); it governs her appearance and takes precedence over the
rest of this bundle on it. What a surface says about her mind is bound by the
[cognition anchor](../cognition/README.md).

## North Star: paper and thread

Her clothes are the system: ivory paper, indigo ink, and a copper thread
running through both, as it runs through the piping and knot buttons she wears.
Paper is where things are written down; the thread is what connects them and
pulls them forward.

## Two registers, one system

- **Stage** (the public site): cinematic, scroll-told, three-dimensional, with
  Nora alive inside it. Award-level ambition: big type, a world you move
  through, an interaction that teaches.
- **Desk** (the portal and the docs): quiet and legible. A place you look
  things up in; motion only confirms.

Both share every token, the type families and the rules of [colour](colour.md)
and [typography](typography.md). The stage may do what the desk may not; the
desk never borrows the stage's motion.

## What the system rejects

Cold slate SaaS greys, gradient blobs and aurora washes, glowing borders,
sparkles, circuitry and "AI" iconography, card grids of features, cute-mascot
pastel candy, and anything that presents Nora as a product rather than as
herself.

## Rules every surface keeps

- Every colour is a scale step with a role, and a new pair is measured before
  it is used.
- zh-Hant and English are at parity on every surface.
- Every word on the stage is real, translatable text with a still fallback.
- Surfaces that show what Nora knows draw from the
  [graph](../cognition/graph.md)'s presentations and the unit boundary; they
  invent no meaning of their own.
