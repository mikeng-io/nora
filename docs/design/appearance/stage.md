# The stage

## The world

The world is the Lingnan mountain-and-sea of her cinematic banner: misty peaks
over a harbour, a stone path climbing to a moon gate, flowering trees. Light
runs from dawn on paper to night in indigo, and the path loops back to dawn. No
neon, no city of circuitry, no digital grid.

## The motifs

The motifs are the [Cognitive Graph](../cognition/README.md) made visible. Each
has one meaning everywhere on the stage.

| motif | means |
|---|---|
| **paper slip**, with a short line and a name | something that was said or happened: an item |
| **thread** | what connects things: pale indigo for understanding, copper for what will happen |
| **lantern**, with a "done when ..." tag | something Nora has committed to do: an intention and its closing condition |
| **logbook** | what she has learned from how things turned out: her lessons |
| **pavilion** | a circle of people (you, a family, a team, friends): a unit; valleys keep them apart |

A lantern lights only while Nora holds it. Without her, slips and threads are a
still map; with her, the lanterns burn.

Names on the stage are short and international (Alex, Sam, Mia, Leo, Priya,
Jordan, Noor, Elena) or roles ("Mum"). Placeholder lines stay generic so the
visitor fills in their own errand.

## The landing page

The page tells one story: **Memory remembers. Knowledge understands. Nora
acts.** It shows the difference instead of explaining it.

1. **Hero.** Dawn above the harbour; Nora notices you. "Here, beyond the
   conversation." Most AI waits to be asked; Nora lives in your conversations.
2. **Circles.** The path forks to four pavilions: You, Family, Team, Friends.
3. **Take one: memory.** Without Nora. Slips pile up; asked, one is retrieved;
   the moment passes anyway. Paper and mist only.
4. **Take two: knowledge.** Without Nora. The slips link into a complete,
   motionless constellation; asked, it answers perfectly; nothing moves.
5. **Rewind.** The scene scrubs backwards to the same morning.
6. **Take three: Nora.** She steps in and a lantern lights: a commitment that
   names when it is done. The first copper and warm light of the page.
7. **One conversation moves another.** A slip from another conversation changes
   what her lantern rests on; its time moves. At night she cannot speak, so she
   carries it into tomorrow.
8. **She speaks first, at the right moment,** in the right room. The lantern
   fades warmly; a line goes into her logbook, and the next lantern is set
   differently.
9. **Rooms stay rooms.** Over the mountain: each pavilion with its own
   lanterns; nothing crosses a valley.
10. **Soulbound.** She steps out: every lantern cools to paper. She steps back
    in: they light.
11. **Gate.** First light at the moon gate. Meet Nora; the source on GitHub.

Every claim the page makes is a behaviour the cognition anchor defines, in its
terms: engagement and reflection loops, intentions that close on recorded
outcomes, reconsideration when what they rest on changes, lessons from
verdicts, units as boundaries.
