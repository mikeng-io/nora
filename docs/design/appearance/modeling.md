# Modeling references

## Evidence levels

Canonical art, visual anchors, modeling references, a mesh, a rig and an
accepted animation are different deliverables. A polished image can establish
face, palette, silhouette and motifs without establishing geometry, scale,
projection, anatomy, clothing construction or deformation.

- Front, side and back labels alone do not make a consistent orthographic
  turnaround, and a T-pose illustration alone does not prove hand orientation,
  digit geometry or matching proportions across views. A stylised 3D picture is
  still an illustration.
- The adult Bauhinia anchor and the Mini reference sheets guide modeling. Byte
  and coverage checks prove an inventory's integrity, not visual or
  engineering acceptance.
- Each claim binds to its own artifact and read-back: a modeling-ready
  reference needs internally consistent views and resolved hands, accessories
  and construction; a usable mesh needs inspected geometry and materials; a
  usable rig needs deformation and animation evidence on the surfaces that
  actually consume it.
- Missing calibration or validation limits the claim, not the art. Candidate
  material stays marked as a reference until the evidence exists, and neither
  repository presence, browser acceptance of 2D derivatives nor media ingest
  stands in for mesh, rig or animation acceptance.

## Form and medium boundaries

Mini keeps mascot proportions and its eye language in every modeling treatment;
the adult form keeps adult proportions and the Bauhinia anchor. Shared motifs
never license swapping one form's face, body ratio or identity into the other.

Two-dimensional production art and three-dimensional modeling input derive
from one identity without being interchangeable. Existing modeled assets stay
separate from newer source-art approval. Refinement may resolve construction
and anatomy while preserving the approved face, silhouette, palette and motifs;
a demonstrated geometric or animation constraint calls for an explicit visual
revision, never a silent redesign. Modeling sheets, candidate meshes and human
references create no custody exception of the kind in
[generation](../media/generation.md).
