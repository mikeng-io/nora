# What she can do

What Nora can do in a given room depends on the services the operator has
connected and what that room allows. This page describes the capabilities in
plain terms; the exact list of her tools lives in the code.

## Where she lives

- **Messaging**: Signal, Discord, WhatsApp and Instagram, in group chats and
  private conversations.
- **Voice**: a Discord voice channel, where she listens when woken and
  answers aloud.
- **The portal**: a web page where the people she knows can see what she
  believes about them, and their shared documents and files.

Each of these is a door into the same Nora. A voice channel is another way
into its room, not a separate memory.

## Understanding people

- Knows who is who across apps, as the operator has mapped them.
- Builds a profile of each person, room and circle from evidence: facts,
  habits and preferences, and guesses she can test.
- Keeps track of plans, open questions and what each conversation was in the
  middle of.
- Notices patterns: a room getting quieter, a topic coming back, someone
  usually being online at a certain time.

## Following through

- Reminders, at a time or when something happens.
- Promises on someone's behalf, followed until something real closes them.
- Follow-ups she decides on herself, delivered only if they still make sense
  when the moment comes.
- Watching outside sources while they matter, such as a page or a price.

## Planning together

- Events with RSVPs.
- Reading polls and their results.
- Shared documents she writes and revises with the group.

## Getting around

- Places, routes, public transport, parking and weather.
- Where people are, or have been, only with their consent and only to those
  the rules allow.
- Saved places, such as "home", used for routes without the location reaching
  the AI model or an outside service.

## Finding things out

- Web search, reading pages and a browser for longer research.
- Public posts on X, Instagram and LinkedIn.
- Who is online, playing a game or in a voice channel.
- Images, voice notes and videos people share, which she can look at or
  listen to.

## Making things

- Images, short videos and GIFs.
- Documents.
- Files and results on her computer workspace.

## Doing longer work

- **A computer workspace** of her own, where files and work carry over from
  one conversation to the next.
- **Helper agents** that take on a long job in the background and report the
  result when it is done.

Finishing the work and telling the room are separate outcomes: a job can
succeed while the moment to mention it passes, and both are recorded.

## Music and voice

- Plays music in a Discord voice channel: queue, skip, volume, what is playing.
- Talks aloud in her own voice.

## Knowing herself

- Can say which version of herself is running and what changed in recent
  releases.
- Keeps a profile of how she has come across, built from how people responded
  to her, never from her own words alone.
