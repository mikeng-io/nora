# V5

## Summary

V4 made Nora a proper member of the places she lives in. V5 is the step to a
persistent member of one circle: she is in the circle's rooms on four
platforms and a Discord voice channel, she perceives it across time scales
(where people are and who is online, what was said and shown, each room's
position, the group's open questions, what each room has become), and what she
may do with what she knows is governed apart from what she can see.

**Four platforms, one circle.** WhatsApp joined Signal and Discord through a
containerised bridge, and Instagram group chats became a fourth room adapter:
she hears them, knows who is in them, threads her replies onto the message she
is answering, and her @-mentions ping on Discord and Instagram instead of going
out as plain text. Message edits are now read as corrections on all four
networks, including WhatsApp edits made on a phone; a reply that quotes a
message older than the transcript window is no longer misread as a new one.
Every turn now tells her which room she is in, which other rooms her group has
and whether each network is up; `room_list` and `room_read` let her say how busy
each room is and read the group's room on another network, while other groups
are only ever said to exist.

**She can read more of the world the friends live in.** Asked about someone, she
can read their Instagram profile, a post or reel with what each photo and video
shows, or their story, under the operator's rule that an account is readable
only if it is public or she follows it, and a story only for someone in her
rooms. X and LinkedIn got their own read and search tools (replacing
`social_fetch`), and a link-owner registry routes a pasted Instagram, X or
LinkedIn link to the tool that owns it. `web_fetch` reads a page as it is today
rather than a search engine's cached copy, and a research turn that runs out of
budget answers from what it found instead of apologising.

**The media read lane.** Every photo, video, sticker and voice note is read once
on arrival, so what people send is part of the record rather than an empty
marker. She now hears video and audio: a clip's sound reaches the describer and
speech in it is transcribed, long videos are read in 90-second chunks instead of
only the first two minutes, and a reading is cached so asking twice does not
watch it twice. Voice notes use a Cantonese-tuned Whisper model.

**Where people are, and whether they are around.** `where_been` answers where
someone has been today, their usual places, weekly rhythm and repeated routes,
under the same disclosure rule as `where_is`, and never invents a home from a
pattern. A defect that split a day spent in one place into half-hourly stays,
skewing every duration and usual arrival time, was fixed; she now says where
the record ends when tracking stops. `online_now` reads Steam and Discord
presence (status, game, music, voice channel), and says which half it could not
check.

**Discord voice.** Saying 「喂 Nora」 in any voice channel of a server reaches
her, what is said lands in that server's text room like a typed message, and she
answers aloud; she can hold voice on several servers at once. Music moved onto
one audio controller that shares the connection with her ear, starts at 25%
volume, can announce into a dedicated channel, and is controlled by asking or by
slash command. A run of wedges that left the room silent was fixed, and a
music failure now raises an alert.

**What she holds over time.** Standing became the room's compact: it keeps the
threads she is holding, knows its own age, carries a small working set of
findings not yet given to the room, and keeps a history instead of being
overwritten. The note tier is now written by background passes: note-candidates
proposes entries, a note is identified by its title rather than its date, a
revision appends rather than replaces, and the curator runs daily and retires
notes whose day has passed. The per-reply debrief, the main source of
duplicate notes, no longer creates any. The culture tier folds each room into a
running account of what that room is, and every turn and background pass reads
it. The knowledge (fact) tier and the room-language tier, both off in V4, were
deleted along with their tools and prompt blocks.

**She may raise something on her own.** The proactive pass reads what a group
has left open and may set itself a reminder to raise it in the room the operator
names as that group's home. It runs when a note or Standing changes and the
rooms have gone quiet, with a once-a-day floor; it must consult her memory of
the people involved before booking a reminder, and books at most one per note.
Participation (speaking when a room goes quiet) and the relevance gate that
would admit it are built and measured, and are off in this deployment.

**A face and a voice of her own.** Her identity now says she has a form, a small
stylised 3D mascot; an image-generation skill tells her when she is in the
picture and uses her canonical reference, and pictures she is not in no longer
take on her look. Her doctrine was rewritten so she stops writing em dashes and
sounds less like an assistant when she names, teases or signs off. Reaction GIF
replies were built and are off by operator decision.

**Governance and security.** Every tool now explains its result when it returns
it rather than in a long description read on every message, and a call with an
undeclared argument is refused instead of silently trimmed. Seven security
fixes: a display name or a newline can no longer forge a line from another
member or from Nora; the gate that admits her uninvited no longer believes a
forged transcript row; the note tier escapes group text and never sends a phone
number to the model provider; `search_memory_workspace` left the room tool
catalogue; and Sentry stopped capturing unredacted frame locals. Operators
gained alerting, working traces, and an eval suite that drives the deployed
Nora against real rooms at real past moments.

**The honest gaps.** Standing still leaves out findings she has not sent
(#445), and its fold dropped a same-day plan that was still open in at least 10
of 206 measured folds (#555). The proactive pass never wakes on a change in
where people are or what they post. Note-merge runs in shadow and only records
what it would fold; duplicates the debrief already made stay until they retire.
The Instagram public summons is built and enabled in dry-run, so it posts
nothing. Threads is parked. Deleted or unsent messages are not captured, and a
number spoken in a clip can still appear in its description, because leaving it
out is a request to the model rather than a filter. Grants are room-shaped, so
"this person may be looked up from rooms they are not in" cannot be expressed
yet.

The full itemised list is in the fragments consumed by this release, archived
under `release-fragments/released/V5/`.
