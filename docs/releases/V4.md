# V4

## Summary

The release that made Nora a proper member of the places she lives in: she now
keeps a running position in each conversation, plans gatherings with RSVPs,
plays Discord music conversationally, and carries a much more careful memory of
who said what and whether it was ever checked.

**Discord music grew up.** Beyond the slash-command bot from V3's window, Nora
can now be asked to play, skip, stop or check music in plain chat — "play apt",
"skip this", "what's playing" — and for a vague request she can search the web
for a real playlist first, then play it as ordinary multi-tool reasoning. The
queue can be paged, topped, expanded from a playlist link and resumed after a
restart as long as someone is still in the voice channel; tracks crossfade
instead of hard-cutting (on by default at 3s); when the queue empties she seeds
a YouTube Mix recommendation instead of going idle, prewarming it so there is
no gap at the track end; volume is controllable; and every slash-command reply
is silent so a music request never pings the room. Session state — now playing,
paused, volume, which channels — persists in Postgres rather than living only
in the process.

**Discord also carries more of her surfaces.** The portal now hosts the tools
catalog, an expandable mobile menu (replacing an 8px-label bottom bar), a
user-scoped memory showcase, doctrine pages, and event pages that respect who
created or RSVP'd to them. Nora can take custody of Discord photos, videos and
voice notes — not just stickers — so a real Discord photo can be described the
way Signal's already are. Drop's public links moved under her own domain. The
Games Hub reached Pulse's real REST API and understands both container-backed
and local-save game types, with a deterministic avatar per player.

**Memory became evidence-bearing.** "Who said that" is now recorded, not just
what was said; a contradiction marks a claim disputed rather than deleting it;
a claim keeps the room's own words even once she works out what they refer to;
short names resolve to the same person as long ones; and everything she returns
from a lookup is presented as data, not as an order. `search_memory` no longer
refuses to search a group's history because one account in the room is unmapped
— it filters, and she says so. She only digs in on claims she actually checked,
and stands by answers she actually looked up. The one load-bearing guard that
was removed: the send-path misattribution veto (R-85) is gone after a live
false refusal, and R-18's model-name veto is disabled by operator decision —
both are now recorded as decisions rather than silently enforced, with the
prose-level rule and its egress review still in place.

**A working position in every conversation (Standing).** After every completed
turn — success, deliberate silence, veto, timeout alike — a second model
reviews the finished exchange and revises one paragraph about where the
conversation currently stands, which is injected at the top of the next turn.
It reads the prior position before writing the next (a contradiction would
accrete otherwise), refuses to overflow, and every cost is bounded and billed
to the person queued behind the conversation, never to the person just
answered. It is ON in this deployment. The fold is deliberately not traced
(the counters are the record) and its reasoning effort is intentionally
unmeasured ("none" as the chosen starting point, with a named spike to measure
later).

**The Dreamer became a real research agent — and mostly sits off.** The
Dreamer runtime is ON: a bounded read-only tool loop per adaptor that screens
everything before writing, stamps every row with the instruction digest and
model that produced it, and can never act — the grantable set is derived from
each tool's declared read/act property, and a loop that cannot stop itself
cannot run. The fact tier's adaptors and the language consolidator remain OFF
(honest earlier failures are recorded, not glossed). The note tier's curator
and revisitor run on this runtime: notes whose question is settled or whose
moment has passed stop appearing, and unsettled plans get written down so the
notebook can start from empty.

**Gatherings, end to end.** Nora can plan dinners and outings through chat —
create, update, RSVP, cancel, list, suggest a venue — with venue details
resolved once via the existing Places infra and cached, and notifications that
actually deliver (the event sentinel that made gathering reminders dead on
arrival in the same window was fixed here).

**The web portal's memory show became private.** A logged-in person now sees
only their own representation, reversing the original design. On the policy
side, the retention sweep is a switch and Nora's own deployment keeps the full
conversation record.

**The honest gaps are recorded alongside everything above.** Standing's
related outputs (per-turn verdict, fact flow) are not built; the watermark
read has a bounded stall under sparse fixes; unguarded reply-path protection is
back to being prose-only; the portal's OAuth apps and tunnel are not yet
publicly reachable; and the voice-note egress ceiling relies on the parser
flagging voice notes correctly. Each is written down with its reversal or
finishing condition.

The full itemised list is in the fragments consumed by this release, archived
under `release-fragments/released/V4/`.
