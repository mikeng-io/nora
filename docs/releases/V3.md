# V3

## Summary

The release that gave Nora a second platform and the ability to hand back a
file instead of a wall of chat.

**Discord, alongside Signal.** Nora now runs every platform she is configured
for at once, and answers each message in the room it was asked in. Getting that
right was most of the work: a reply, a 👀, and a reminder all have to go back to
the platform they came from, and an early version that listened on Discord
while still replying through Signal would have answered a question asked in one
room in front of a different set of people. Her health page reports each
platform separately and calls her unwell if she has gone deaf on any one of
them, rather than only checking the first.

**Files.** Ask for something that wants to be a file — a report, a chart, a
rendered map — and she builds it in a sandboxed container and hands back a
link. Quick jobs come back in the same reply; a long one gets an honest "I'm
working on it" and then arrives later as its own message, in her own words,
without anybody having to ask again. A file has to earn being a file: if it
would read exactly the same pasted into the chat, she reshapes it around what
the data actually is instead of putting headers on the same words.

**Her own link service.** Short links now come from Nora's own drop service
rather than the old Worker, and an artifact can be revised in place while
keeping the link that was already shared.

**Identity got stricter.** A person is now recognised only by something they
cannot quietly change — a phone number or a user ID. Usernames are still
written down so a human can read the file, but they never decide who anybody
is: a rename, or somebody claiming a handle its previous owner gave up, can no
longer turn one person into another.

**Asking where someone is** now works for anyone who shares their location with
her, not only people who happen to be in the group you are asking from. Who may
ask is unchanged, and so is who may be told.

Two changes from the V1→V2 window are recorded here rather than lost: her
register in a row, and `search_memory` learning to say "I found things I may not
repeat" instead of "nobody said anything". They shipped before this release and
V2's record covered only the GIF feature; each is marked as backfilled in its
own fragment.

The full itemised list is in the fragments consumed by this release, archived
under `release-fragments/released/V3/`.
