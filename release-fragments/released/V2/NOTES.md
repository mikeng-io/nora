New:
- Nora can send described, PG-rated Giphy reaction GIFs in Signal.
  - GIFs can be sent alone or with a caption.
  - Nora selects from Giphy title, description, and tag metadata rather than sending a random result.
Limitations:
- Nora does not inspect GIF frames.
- Signal stickers and other chat platforms are not included.
- GIF search terms are sent to Giphy; Nora should use only a generic visual reaction phrase.
