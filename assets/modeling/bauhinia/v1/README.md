## Limitations and acceptance

- Hands and individual finger separations remain unresolved in the sheet art.
- The side-view arm is drawn end-on. Labels, guide lines and T-pose titles do
  not prove exact orthographic projection, matched scale or cross-view
  alignment. No exact coordinates or body measurements are established.
- Small flower geometry and petal counts, clasp construction and cuff
  placements vary across views. A five-petal Bauhinia motif is the design
  intention; labels claiming exact counts are not geometric evidence.
- Adult and Mini have distinct eye shapes. Shared eye language does not mean
  identical facial geometry, nor does a PNG prove eye rigging or expressions.
- Coat layers, hidden surfaces, material separation, mesh topology, scale,
  rigging, deformation and animation remain unvalidated. These sheets are
  not model-ready and establish no actual 3D model.

## Earlier v1 modeling handoff — historical

**Do not submit a whole layout sheet as a single character to image-to-3D.**
After user acceptance, prepare isolated, validated per-view exports for one
form/treatment at a time, in consistent **front → right → back** order. Check
projection, scale/alignment, hands and outfit details before any paid Meshy
step. The crop/export workflow and those isolated inputs are still pending;
no Meshy call has run for this pack.

The v1 integration imported existing artwork. The subsequent preparatory phase
added policy/source requirements without generation or checks. Later v2 sheets
and isolated inputs are documented in the v2 pack; their existence does not
validate the geometry gaps above. The earlier parent gate covers the original
14 staged files only, not the later 2D integration tree.
Inventory success measures bytes and coverage, not aesthetic acceptance or
model readiness. The approved adult direction holds unless the operator
changes it or a demonstrated modeling/animation constraint requires an
explicit revision.
