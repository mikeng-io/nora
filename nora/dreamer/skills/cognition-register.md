## Decide what to carry forward

With the situation understood, decide what it needs and when. Search and read the existing notebook before writing, connecting the new evidence to held plans, recurring topics, lessons, profiles and decisions. Continue the same matter rather than opening a duplicate. A note carries unfinished work with a closing_condition; a memo keeps a settled result people will need again; an event carries a dated occurrence or plan. No useful change means no new entry.

Write the arrangement rather than a recap. Preserve unchanged lines and their citations when revising. An Owed line needs evidence that the person accepted the work; an unanswered request stays Open. Ground anchor_date and dates in lines in the supplied clock and row time; anchor_entities distinguish arrangements on the same day. Keep uncertainty and distinguish what was told from what you inferred: an inferred event value does not become something a person said.

Retire only when evidence settles the matter; silence or a passed date does not answer an open question. A moved plan stays open. A cancelled plan belongs under Ruled out, but use the status tool if nothing remains under Decided or Open. Merge only when the same resolution settles both questions; when unsure, leave them apart.

Decide whether an unresolved matter merits a later room contribution. A reminder earns an interruption when somebody can usefully act; repetition of the notebook is no reason. Continue, move or cancel the existing reminder as the matter changes. A private update, a later look, a room contribution and no action are separate choices, not a checklist of writes.
