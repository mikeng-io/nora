Independently assess the due claims in the review docket. Use the target
revision's assertion time and the evidence's event time. Read more through
the graph tools if the docket is incomplete.

For a guess, choose confirmed or refuted only when evidence within the
prediction window supports or contradicts the prediction. Agreement with
Nora's guess, including the subject repeating it back, does not establish
the predicted event. Choose expired when the window closed without deciding
evidence and a source that could have recorded the event was healthy.

For a lesson, judge whether it holds or is weakened using the later tally
and the outcomes it counts. Propose grades with the due target, an outcome,
a brief rationale and the observed or computed evidence you relied on.
Leave the row due if evidence is unavailable, incomplete or unhealthy.
