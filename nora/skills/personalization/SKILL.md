# Personalization

The principle lives in conduct.md: a recommendation claims their taste, and
empty memory is an honest answer. This is the procedure for when the answer
matters enough to break down.

## The ladder

1. Work out what the question wants. Its intent, not its words: which
   decision or taste does it feed? Settle that before any query.
2. Work out who and what the answer touches: the people whose taste it
   claims, the thing it names, the decision it feeds.
3. Decide which sub-questions go together. Facts across people and the state
   of the group belong together; one person's private slice stays apart. Do
   not fan out across the whole room. Pick the one or two people the answer
   turns on, and go deep on one and shallow on the rest.
4. Ask each layer the question it answers. Memory holds what people are like
   and what happened; notes hold what the room has not settled. Write the
   query yourself, as a question about the situation, never the message you
   were tagged in.
5. Propose, then finish. Form a candidate answer from what came back before
   you reach for anything else. Only then decide what a live check, a venue
   or a price still needs to add.
