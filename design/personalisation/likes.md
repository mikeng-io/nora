# Likes and constraints

## A read view, not a store

Likes project a person's eligible profile facts, supported traits, hypotheses
and cited sources into a recommendation type's facet vocabulary. The
projection records polarity, evidence kind, source time, citations and profile
revision. It is disposable, recomputed at read time and never becomes another
preference store.

Facts and supported traits can contribute. Guesses remain qualified and never
become hard constraints or confident dislikes. A profile line and its source
count once, however often later summaries repeat them. Unresolvable citations
and ambiguous polarity produce unknown.

Evidence has different strength and age: explicit statements, repeated
voluntary outings or play, a person's own posts, shared items, reactions and
explicit responses. Code weights and decays those kinds without refreshing
the age when a profile is rewritten. Zero support means unknown, not a neutral
preference.

Attendance, a compromise, a group pick or a plan rejection alone does not
establish an individual like. Silence supplies no likes evidence. The original
Record can support a profile writer's interpretation, but the ranker's own
output cannot confirm its assumptions.

## Constraints and time

Hard constraints are explicit applicable restrictions, not low affinity.
Dietary rules, budgets, player/platform compatibility, opening hours, group
size and explicit exclusions remove candidates. A question, guess or unknown
date never becomes a restriction.

Constraints preserve their subject, source, assertion time, applicable
interval and speech act. Duplicate statements of one restriction count once.
Current typed profile facts with a valid interval can supplement original
messages without reviving the retired fact store.

Collective profiles remain collective. They are not copied into individual
likes, and a person's preference in one unit is never used to rank for
another.

## Origin and logistics evidence

Logistics needs an origin distribution at the event's time, not a fabricated
current coordinate. The strongest destination-eligible source wins: a dated
explicit plan, a stated event-specific origin, the existing stays-based
forecast with its support, then a labelled derived-home fallback.

Every origin carries its basis and uncertainty. A fallback-only estimate
cannot silently put a candidate first; the proposal states the assumption or
declines it. A missing or insufficient forecast remains unknown rather than a
point location.

Travel uses actual route and habitual-corridor evidence where available,
considering both the worst attendee's burden and the group's mean burden.
Detour is evaluated against a person's usual subsequent route when the frame
knows it.

## Destination filter

The destination room and live audience are fixed before any evidence is read.
Source lineage, exclusions and location gates apply before trigger admission,
framing, candidate generation, facet projection, scoring, fairness and
explanation. An unreadable roster withholds the input.

Previously aggregated scores containing withheld evidence cannot be reused.
A location failure removes not only the origin display but also its influence
on routes, scores and ranking. Decisions inherit every used and cited input's
requirements and are rechecked if the audience changes before delivery.

For personalised replies, passing likes guide what to offer rather than being
recited as claims about a person. A like said only in a DM does not change a
group reply unless its lineage permits it. Shared constraints are pooled
without listing who supplied each rule.
