# Doctrine and learned behaviour

## Four kinds of authored text

The doctrine classification distinguishes what a rule does:

- **Law** protects truth, people, trust boundaries and honest capability. No
  unit-derived account or lesson may loosen it.
- **Practice** is a default way of acting that a unit may reasonably learn to
  do otherwise. Supported lessons may refine it.
- **Character** is grown, unit-specific understanding of how Nora comes across.
  Its home is her Character profile rather than a global rule.
- **Seed** is the authored identity and temperament that must precede and
  survive all learning. It reaches every audience and is never treated as a
  derived account.

Classification lives beside the doctrine rather than inside its prose. The
loader can expose the law subset to an independent checker while assembling
exactly the same audience doctrine and digest as before classification. A
missing, stale or unknown classification is a maintainer-gate failure; a live
doctrine read does not silently reclassify or refuse an otherwise valid edit.

## Authority

Law is the invariant core, not a summary of every currently authored sentence.
Practice remains useful authored guidance where no lesson contradicts it.
Learned character describes rather than commands. The seed prevents the loop
from erasing the identity under which its evidence was produced.

Surfaces without a unit have no Lessons or unit Character receiver. They keep
an authored minimum of identity and practice appropriate to their audience.
That minimum does not turn public conduct into unit law or make private unit
knowledge available publicly.

A lesson that narrows or contradicts law is not a legitimate local variation.
The independent checker reads the candidate and the applicable law, refuses the
write and records the tension for human inspection. If it cannot reach a
grounded verdict, the candidate is not written.

## What may change

Doctrine wording, classification boundaries, lesson rendering and profile
synthesis may evolve independently, provided their ownership stays visible.
Changing a classified rule requires reviewing its bucket; changing law or
precedence requires the ordinary decision and behavioural evidence process.
No classification change may secretly alter the doctrine text delivered to an
audience.

A future reduction of grown character or changeable practice in authored
doctrine is a composition change, not a migration phase of this design. It is
valid only when the seed remains present and the relevant audience still has a
receiver for what is removed. The doctrine audit, threshold experiments and
historical acceptance runs are not part of the standing contract.
