# Self growth

Nora has an authored identity and can also learn how she comes across and how
she should act in each unit. Growth is derived, cited and revisable; it never
becomes a second author of identity, law or trust.

**Status:** built

[Open issues](https://github.com/mikeng-io/nora/issues?q=is%3Aopen+%22design%2Fself-growth%2F%22)

## Parts

- **The identity seed** is authored doctrine: her name, AI nature, maker, form
  and temperament. It reaches every audience and is not displaced by learned
  accounts.
- **Character profiles** are bounded syntheses of a room, unit, person or Nora,
  written from evidence within one unit. [Character](character.md) defines
  their evidence, revision and rendering; [profiles](../unified-memory/profiles.md)
  defines their facts, traits, guesses and disputes.
- **Response episodes** pair one of her acts with the replies, reactions, edits
  and inferred follows that closed its observation window. They let her learn
  from consequences rather than from her own prose alone.
- **Lessons** are cited, per-unit practices induced across response episodes or
  offered by an explicit correction. [Lessons](lessons.md) owns their writes,
  independent doctrine check, decay and operator rollback.
- **The doctrine classification** separates law, changeable practice, grown
  character and the permanent seed without changing the doctrine text.
  [Doctrine](doctrine.md) defines precedence and the independent check.

## Terms and promises

A profile says what Nora understands; a lesson says how she should act. Neither
is a transcript summary, and neither may cite only her own sends as evidence of
how others receive her. One writer owns each account or lesson revision chain.
Memory search may guide investigation but is not itself citable evidence.

The unit is the read boundary. Accounts and lessons combine evidence across its
rooms, so their rendering follows the unit's disclosure policy rather than a
lineage filter. They never widen tool, action, location or audience authority.

Law always wins. A supported lesson may refine authored practice, while a
lesson that loosens law is refused and preserved only as an operator-visible
tension. A failed check cannot pass a write. Public surfaces without a unit
retain their authored identity and practice because no unit-derived block can
represent them.

## Change and failure

Thresholds, storage, prompt composition and objective wording may change without
changing these boundaries. New evidence must remain attributable to the unit
and entity it concerns, and every derived write must retain its provenance.

An unsettled session does not advance its evidence watermark. Repeated failure
leaves the prior account or lessons intact. Missing derived state renders no
empty block, and a failed derived read does not prevent a turn. Operator
retirement and rollback remain authoritative over later learning from older
evidence.
