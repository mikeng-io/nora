# Lessons

## Learning from outcomes

A lesson is a per-unit practice supported by what happened after Nora acted.
The unit of evidence is a closed response episode: her send together with the
quoted replies, reactions, edits and separately identified inferred follows in
its observation window. An open episode cannot teach a settled pattern.

The Lessons Dreamer session wakes on accumulated closed episodes, a lesson due
for reconsideration or an explicit correction addressed to Nora. It reads the
new episodes and active lessons, then writes, reinforces, consolidates or
retires through checked verbs. A session must settle with a write, retirement
or explicit no-change outcome.

A correction from a person can ground a lesson without waiting for the ordinary
multi-episode pattern. Detection addresses Nora through a quote, structured
mention or her name and recognises an instruction about her own behaviour. It
does not create a new public stimulus or action grant.

Ordinary learned lessons require support from distinct episodes. Whether an
unanswered send asked something is judged from its actual words, not from a
model-authored flag. Inferred follows remain distinguishable from quoted
replies so the writer can weigh weaker evidence appropriately.

## Checked writes

Every write passes the same boundaries regardless of which session requested
it:

- citations resolve to evidence shown to that session and satisfy the echo rule;
- identifier and coordinate screens prevent derived text from becoming an
  egress path;
- a new lesson does not merely restate law or another active lesson;
- the unit's bounded lesson budget is respected, requiring consolidation or
  retirement rather than unlimited growth;
- an independent model call judges the candidate against the law that reaches
  unit occasions;
- an operator's retirement or rollback prevents relearning from evidence no
  newer than that decision.

The doctrine check is separate from the writer and uses a different model. A
conflict must identify a law rule actually shown to the checker and explain the
conflict. Malformed, cut-off or unavailable verdicts refuse the write. A
confirmed conflict is recorded as an append-only tension for the operator;
tensions never render to Nora or become tool results.

Lessons decay when later evidence no longer supports them. Operator retirement
is deliberate and durable; automatic decay is not permission to relearn an
operator-refuted practice from the same old episodes.

## Rendering and precedence

Active lessons render through declared context assembly on room and proactive
occasions where the unit's derived self-knowledge may be shown. They contain no
record identifiers or citations. A lesson may go ahead of authored practice,
but never ahead of law; the block's own description and the write-time check
preserve that distinction.

A lesson changes practice, not identity, trust, capabilities or delivery
authority. It does not reach public surfaces without a unit, and it is not a
second doctrine writer. Cognition may use learned understanding through its
normal declared context and tools without creating another lesson store.

## Failure behaviour

Failed citation, screening, capacity or doctrine checks commit nothing. A
doctrine-check outage is `unavailable`, never consistency. An unsettled session
holds its evidence for bounded retry, while an actual write is a positive
outcome rather than `nothing_found`.

A failed lesson read omits the block without failing the turn. Missing lessons
mean no derived practice, not permission to invent one. The previous validated
revision chain and operator tensions remain available for diagnosis and
rollback.
