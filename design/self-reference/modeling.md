# Modeling references

## Evidence levels

Canonical art, visual anchors, modeling references, a mesh, a rig and an
accepted animation are different deliverables. A polished PNG can establish
face, palette, silhouette and motifs without establishing geometry, scale,
projection, anatomy, clothing construction or deformation.

The adult Bauhinia anchor and Mini reference sheets guide modeling. Front,
side and back labels alone do not prove a consistent orthographic turnaround,
and a T-pose illustration alone does not prove hand orientation, digit
geometry or matching proportions across views. A stylized 3D PNG is still an
illustration.

The visual-asset inventory records masters, derivative recipes, consumers and
retained history. It keeps accepted source art distinct from generated web
copies, shader textures and older exports. Byte and coverage checks prove
inventory integrity, not visual or engineering acceptance.

## Form and medium boundaries

Mini keeps mascot proportions and its current eye language in every modeling
treatment. The adult form uses adult proportions and the approved Bauhinia
anchor. Shared motifs do not license swapping one form's face, body ratio or
identity into the other.

Two-dimensional production art and three-dimensional modeling inputs may be
derived from the same identity without being interchangeable deliverables.
Existing modeled assets remain separate from newer source-art approval. No
canonical self custody exception is created for modeling sheets, candidate
meshes or human references.

Modeling refinement may resolve construction and anatomy while preserving the
approved face, silhouette, palette and motifs. A demonstrated geometric or
animation constraint requires an explicit visual revision rather than a silent
redesign.

## Acceptance and failure

A modeling-ready reference needs internally consistent views and resolved
hands, accessories and construction. A usable mesh needs inspected geometry
and materials; a usable rig needs deformation and animation evidence on its
actual consumer surfaces. Each claim binds to its own artifact and read-back.

Missing calibration or validation limits the claim; it does not make the
source art unusable as art. Candidate material remains clearly marked as a
reference until the corresponding evidence exists. Repository presence,
browser acceptance of 2D derivatives and media ingest do not substitute for
mesh, rig or animation acceptance.
