# Learning

Nora has learned when an outcome of her own act changes a later decision and
every link of that chain is in the graph.

## The chain

| link | in the graph | written by |
|---|---|---|
| **outcome** | the verdict on an intention | code |
| **revision** | a lesson or claim created or revised, citing verdicts | model proposes, code admits |
| **retrieval** | the lesson or track record pushed into a comparable decision | code, by prefetch |
| **declared dependence** | the new intention's rests-on edge to it | model proposes, code admits |
| **use** | the lesson or track record changed the later decision | an independent judge, reading the later input, decision and act |

Each link needs its own evidence: a write proves persistence, being shown
proves availability, a rests-on edge proves only that the model declared a
dependence. Use is established only by an independent judge that reads what
the later pass was given, what it decided and what it did. A later good result
alone does not prove the revision caused it.

## Forms

| form | is | basis | exists |
|---|---|---|---|
| **verdict** | the outcome of one achieve intention: achieved, dropped, leaked or unknown | computed | when it closes |
| **track record** | the verdicts of one class of intention | computed | at read time |
| **lesson** | how to act in a unit, citing several verdicts or one correction; an affiliate also reads its primary's, its own first | inferred, induced | stored with provenance; revised as evidence changes |
| **reconsideration** | an intention due again because a row it rests on was revised | committed, by the rests-on edge | when that row changes |

- The **class** of an intention is computed: its form, who it is owed to, its
  unit and the type of its closing condition. A model-made similarity score
  would make the track record a judgement.
- An unknown verdict counts toward no track record, lesson or tally.
- Only computed forms are derived afresh at read time; a lesson is written
  once and revised, never re-inferred from raw history on every pass.

## Lessons are graded by their consequences

A decision resting on a lesson makes its verdict evidence about that lesson.
Code keeps each lesson's **support and contradiction** from the verdicts of the
achieve intentions resting on it, and for a maintain intention, of its
children. A lesson whose contradiction outruns its support is
put before the next wake pass to revise or retire. A lesson the
operator retired is not relearned from evidence no newer than that retirement.

## Outcome levels

| level | means | used for |
|---|---|---|
| **delivered** | the act's outcome item reports it done | delivery accounting |
| **acknowledged** | any response | reception counts only |
| **accepted** | an explicit pick, agreement or acceptance | the usual done-when of a suggestion |
| **beneficial** | what it was for happened: they played it, they went | what learning ultimately grades, where it can be observed |

A suggestion's verdict follows acceptance or benefit, never acknowledgement,
so Nora learns to make suggestions worth taking, not to raise her reply rate.

## Signals, strongest first

1. An identity's correction aimed at Nora: enough alone to ground a lesson.
2. A verdict: whether what she set out to do happened.
3. Reception — replies, reactions, edits: how an act was received, never
   whether it was right.
4. Nora's own words ground nothing beyond that she said them.

Learning must not optimise agreement or engagement over grounded
understanding.

## Evidence of learning

| measure | is | from | produced by |
|---|---|---|---|
| **verdict coverage** | the share of achieve intentions that close with a known verdict | intentions and verdicts | a read over the graph |
| **loose share** | the share of intentions with untyped conditions | intentions | a read over the graph |
| **declared dependence** | the share of decisions resting on a lesson or track record | rests-on edges | a read over the graph |
| **use** | the share of sampled decisions an independent judge finds were changed by what they rest on | sampled passes' inputs, decisions and acts | evaluation, by a judge |
| **efficacy** | the achieved rate of intentions resting on a lesson against comparable ones resting on none — an association, never proof of cause | verdicts and rests-on edges | a read over the graph |
| **adaptation** — the headline measure | the share of sampled passes whose decision changes, judged semantically, when the same recorded pass is replayed without its learned rows (lessons, track records, verdicts), beyond the variation of replaying it unchanged | recorded passes, replayed in matched repeated draws of both arms | evaluation, by replay |

Adaptation separates what Nora learned from what retrieval found. A change
counts only when an independent semantic comparison finds the decisions differ
and the difference exceeds the instability of replaying the pass unchanged,
under otherwise matched conditions and evaluation's repeated-draw rules. Below
that it is **sensitivity**, not learning. It measures the influence of learned
rows at one recorded decision, not the whole journey from acquisition to
correction. A replay that cannot reproduce the original input is unavailable,
never a pass.

A read over the graph reports unavailable when a source it needs is unhealthy.
Counts of passes, admissions, refusals and debrief outcomes are instruments
with their three states; rates are reads, not counters.

Missing, truncated or unmatched evidence is an error, never a pass. The writer
never grades its own learning.

**The reading that would reopen this**: verdict coverage stays low because
decide rarely writes a typed condition; then learning is no stronger than
reception.
