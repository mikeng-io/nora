# Boundaries

Boundaries are what no content of the graph can change: the authority of
authored text, the scope of every row, and what may leave Nora.

## Authority

| text | basis | may be changed by |
|---|---|---|
| **law**: truth, people, trust boundaries, honest capability; one for every agent | authored | the operator only |
| **seed**: an agent's authored name, identity and temperament; one per agent | authored | the operator only |
| **practice**: default ways of acting | authored | the operator; a unit's lessons may refine it |
| **lessons** and Nora's **profile** | inferred | evidence, through the reflection loop |

- Learned content renders below authored doctrine and never loosens law.
- A lesson that narrows or contradicts law is refused by an independent
  checker reading the applicable law; the conflict is recorded as a tension
  with operator exposure. An unreachable verdict refuses the write.
- Surfaces without a unit have no lessons and no unit profile; they keep an
  authored minimum of seed and practice.

## Agents

An **agent** is one Nora: an authored profile, with its own seed and its own
platform accounts, responsible for one social circle. The circle is its
primary unit; the sub-groups inside it are its affiliates ([graph](graph.md#scope)).
Anyone outside the circle belongs to another agent, never to a private silo
inside this one.

- Every agent runs in one deployment: one database, one set of executors and
  brakes. An agent is a scope, never a process or a container.
- An agent speaks only through its own accounts. A room is bound to one unit,
  so to one agent; another agent's account in that room is a stranger to it.
- A person known to two agents is two independent states that never meet;
  only authored identity is shared.

Each agent is an entity like any other. Her acts are items, beliefs about her
are claims, and her commitments are intentions. Her profile and lessons need
people's responses to her acts, never only her own earlier words.

## Scope

- The **unit** is the read boundary. Two rooms that must not share what is
  said belong to different units.
- The **origin room** binds where a row may render and where an act about it
  lands; a row whose room does not resolve is refused, never redirected.
- **Excluded persons** are never revealed to or through: not in a reply, a
  reminder or its context.
- **Exposure**: external rows may reach a room through the guards; internal
  rows are read by Nora and never egress; operator rows are never read by Nora.
- A person in two units has independent state in each; an affiliate reads
  the primary's state about them, never the reverse.
- **A DM is a room of the receiving agent's primary unit**, the agent whose
  account received it. It reads that unit and each affiliate that lists its
  owner, for an audience of that person only, through the ordinary disclosure
  rules ([private DM](../private-dm/admission.md)). Its raw items are never
  shown outside the DM. A claim citing DM items carries the DM as its origin:
  it may be shown to the model in any room that reads the primary unit, marked
  with that origin, and doctrine governs whether anything drawn from it is
  said there. That rule is doctrine, not a code guarantee: code bounds what the
  model is shown, never a paraphrase.
- **Public surfaces without a unit** — a public comment summons — read and
  write no graph state: no prefetch, no debrief, no claims. They keep their own
  public ledger and an authored minimum of seed and practice.

## Egress

- Model-composed deliveries come only from the engagement loop, through the
  tag pipeline or the fire path, after the guards run against the current room
  and roster. The other admitted routes — deterministic notifications and
  public publication, with its own ledger — are the ones in [acts](acts.md),
  through the same common guards. Every act passes the act envelope.
- Credential and doctrine leak protection always run.
- Content checks — identifier and coordinate filters, place and person-name
  screens, the location audience subset and unknown-person checks — follow
  the repository's content-check operating mode, off by default
  ([ADR 0030](../../docs/adr/0030-content-filters-are-disabled-by-default-and-reversible.md)).
  When enabled they fail closed. Scope, consent, availability and the
  three-state results never switch off.
- Silence is a valid outcome of every delivering pass.
- An outside query carries only the minimum public question: never a private
  person's name, handle, address, identifier, coordinate or trace.
- A saved place is an entity. Its coordinate may serve a requested route
  inside code; it never reaches model prose or an outside query, and a
  person's live position is never a routing endpoint.
- The Cognitive Graph is Nora's only memory. No third-party memory is fed or
  read.
