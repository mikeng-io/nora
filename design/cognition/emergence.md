# Emergence

A capability is **emergent** when the generic loops produce it over the graph,
with no code path, store, prompt section or loop of its own. Recommendation,
personalisation, follow-through and learning are emergent: they are semantic,
so they must stay something Nora can learn.

A capability that needs a part of its own shows the form is missing something;
the form is extended for every capability, or not at all.

## Closed form, open content

| | closed form | open content |
|---|---|---|
| **is** | axes, kinds, edge types, lifecycles, stages, admission, guards, doctrine | which entities exist, what Nora believes, which concerns she keeps, what she watches, what she has learned |
| **changed by** | the operator, in this design and doctrine | evidence, through the loops |

Code can check only what it can name, so the form is fixed; capabilities come
from combining content. Emergent never means unexplained: every concern Nora
originates is a well-formed intention that rests on evidence and closes by a
recorded outcome.

## Code computes form, never meaning

- **Code**: counts, compares, resolves, matches conditions it can evaluate,
  derives baselines and verdicts, enforces scope and disclosure, bounds spend
  and delivery.
- **Model**: what someone likes, what fits a group, how tastes weigh against
  each other, which candidate to propose, when a suggestion is welcome — as
  claims and lessons it revises.
- No domain has an authored facet vocabulary, feature weights, aggregation
  formula or recommendation type in code. A domain needs grounded content:
  items about its candidates, claims about its people, verdicts of what was
  proposed.

**The reading that would reopen this**: a domain's suggestions stay poor
however much evidence accumulates; its vocabulary is then authored as doctrine
Nora reads, never as code.

## Capabilities through the generic loops

| capability | how the generic loops produce it |
|---|---|
| **game recommendations** | items linking the same site rise above the room's baseline — a generic count over observed link metadata (stimulus); orient judges that the room is into those games and claims it; decide opens a maintain intention that watches those games' pages; a sale (watch item) leads to a child intention, "suggest it, done when someone accepts it", delivered by a proactive pass that may stay silent; verdicts build its track record and a lesson about which suggestions the room takes up |
| **following through on a promise** | a called pass commits an achieve intention owed to the identity, done when the booking is confirmed; the confirming item closes it, or its time passes and it leaks |
| **noticing someone has gone quiet** | an identity's activity falls well below its baseline (stimulus); orient looks for a held explanation; with none, decide may open an intention delivered when it comes up, resting on the aggregate; their return closes it |
| **learning a room's timing** | early reminders in a room leak while later ones are achieved; the track record shows it; a lesson forms; the next reminder rests on it and moves its support |
| **choosing dinner for a group** | the plan is a claim; Nora's part is an intention owed to the attendees; decide reads their likes and constraints for that audience and judges fit; the pick is the verdict; an identity's taste is revised only from their own explicit liking or dislike, never from a group pick or compromise |

Each uses only items, aggregates, claims, intentions, edges, the five stages
and their checks.

## Evidence of emergence

- **originated concerns**: maintain intentions Nora opened unasked;
- **their usefulness**: their children's achieved rate against concerns people
  asked for;
- **capability-specific parts**: code paths, stores, prompt sections and loops
  serving one capability — a count that only falls, taken as an inventory of
  the code.

If originated concerns are consistently less useful than requested ones, the
form admits concerns it cannot ground, and the admission rule for maintain
intentions changes.
