# Emergence

## What emergence means

A capability is **emergent** when the generic loop produces it over the graph,
with no code path, store, prompt section or loop of its own. Recommendation,
personalisation, follow-through and learning are capabilities of this kind:
they are semantic, so they cannot be written down once in code and remain
something Nora can learn.

A capability that needs a part of its own shows that the form is missing
something. The form is extended in general, for every capability, or not at
all.

## Closed form, open content

| | closed form | open content |
|---|---|---|
| **is** | the axes, kinds, edges, lifecycles, stages, admission rules, guards, doctrine | which entities exist, what Nora believes, which concerns she keeps, what she watches, what she has learned |
| **changed by** | the operator, in this design and doctrine | evidence, through the loop |
| **why** | code can check only what it can name; admission, verdicts and provenance need a fixed form | capabilities come from combining content, not from extending the form |

Emergence does not mean unexplained. Nora may originate any concern, but only
as a well-formed intention that rests on evidence and closes by an observed
outcome, so every originated concern traces back to the items that started it.

## Code computes form, never meaning

Code counts, compares, resolves, matches conditions it can evaluate, derives
baselines and verdicts, enforces scope and disclosure, and bounds spend and
delivery. It does not decide what someone likes, what fits a group, how one
person's taste weighs against another's, which candidate to propose or when a
suggestion is welcome. Those are claims and lessons the session writes and
revises from evidence.

So no domain has an authored facet vocabulary, feature weights, an aggregation
formula or a recommendation type in code. Each would fix a semantic answer
where Nora cannot revise it. What a domain does need is grounded content:
items about its candidates, claims about the people, and verdicts of what was
proposed.

**The reading that would reopen this:** if a domain's suggestions stay poor
however much evidence accumulates, that domain's vocabulary is authored — as
doctrine Nora reads, never as code.

## Capabilities through the generic loop

**Game recommendations.** People post store links in a room: items. Code's
count of game links in the room crosses its baseline: a stimulus. Orient links
the links to game entities and induces a claim about what the room plays.
Decide opens a maintain intention for the room that holds while the interest
holds, watches those games' store pages, rests on the claim and is reviewed
weekly. A watch returns a sale: an item that bears on the held intention.
Decide opens a child achieve intention — suggest the sale, done when someone
picks it up or replies — that serves the parent. The fire path re-judges the
room at delivery and may stay silent. The reply is an item; verify writes the
child's verdict; the parent's track record grows; a lesson forms about which
suggestions this room takes up.

**Following through on a promise.** Someone asks Nora to check back on a
booking: a conscious turn commits an achieve intention owed to them, done when
the booking is confirmed, delivered at a chosen moment. The confirmation
arrives as an item and closes it, or the time passes and it leaks.

**Noticing someone has gone quiet.** Code's baseline of a person's activity in
a room drops well below normal: a stimulus. Orient checks for an explanation
in what is held — a trip, an injury with a valid-to time. With none, decide
may open an intention delivered when it comes up, resting on the aggregate.
Their return to the room is an item that closes it.

**Learning a room's timing.** Several of Nora's early-morning reminders in one
room leak while later ones are achieved. The track record for that class shows
it; the session induces a lesson; the next reminder there rests on it, and its
verdict moves the lesson's support.

**Choosing dinner for a group.** A plan for a gathering is a claim; Nora's part
is an intention to help choose, owed to the attendees. Decide reads the
attendees' likes for that destination audience, calls a read for candidates,
and judges fit from the claims. The pick, or the absence of one, is the
verdict, and claims about each person's taste are revised from what they chose.

Each uses only items, aggregates, claims, intentions, edges, the five stages and
their checks.

## Evidence of emergence

- **originated concerns** — maintain intentions Nora opened without being asked;
- **their usefulness** — the achieved rate of their children against concerns
  people asked for; emergence is working when originated concerns perform close
  to requested ones;
- **capability-specific parts** — code paths, stores, prompt sections and loops
  that exist for one capability. This count only falls.

Emergent behaviour nobody wanted is drift. If originated concerns are
consistently less useful than requested ones, the form admits concerns it
cannot ground, and the admission rule for maintain intentions is what changes.
