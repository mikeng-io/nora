# 55 · Self

## 1. What cognition is made of

**Cognition is not a tier.** It is two things together:

- **the process** — the loop that observes, digests and acts ([30](30-cognition.md));
- **what the process has built** — everything in Register, Character and
  Standing, accumulated and revised session after session.

The operator's aim (2026-09-28): Nora builds an identity — who she is — not
only from the doctrine she was given and what she observed, but from what she
has **digested and thought about**, and grows from the interactions people
have with her and with each other.

## 2. Her identity is the slice of state that is about her

| layer | what it says about her | kind | changes how |
|---|---|---|---|
| **given** | who she is meant to be: constraints, voice, conduct | Doctrine (authored) | only the operator |
| **done** | what she said and decided, and what happened after | Record (her sends, `loop_decisions`, reminders' outcomes) | every act |
| **digested** | who she has become in this circle, as the circle met her | **Character · Nora** | cognition, from what she did **and how people responded** |
| **held** | what she currently thinks, follows and owes | **Standing** (room, unit, person) | cognition and the tag debrief |
| (heard about her) | a third party's digest of her own words | memory (self card) | Honcho |

**Character · Nora is where the digested self lives.** It is the answer to
"is it the Character tier?": the Character tier holds her identity as one of
its entities, beside persons, rooms and the unit, and cognition is what
writes it. Her full identity is that account read together with the doctrine
above it and the Standing beside it.

## 3. Growth, and how far she may change herself

Every turn reads her Character and Standing ([15](15-map.md) §2, `self`). So
when cognition revises them, her behaviour changes on the next turn, with no
code or doctrine change. Three levels:

| level | what changes | who decides | example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **automatic** | Character · Nora, Standing | cognition, cited, revisable | she learns this circle wants short replies and jokes back; she stops raising a topic that went unanswered twice |
| **proposed** | her conduct and voice doctrine | **she proposes a diff, the operator approves** | "I keep being corrected for over-explaining; propose adding a line to conduct" |
| **never** | constraints (the R-rules, disclosure, safety) | nobody through her | — |

The proposed level makes her growth reach the authored layer without letting
her rewrite it: a proposal is a reviewable change with the evidence that
motivated it, merged or refused like any other.

## 4. What keeps growth honest

- **The echo rule** ([50](50-character-and-standing.md) §3): her own words
  alone never ground a change to her Character. A change needs people's
  responses: an answer, a correction, a laugh, silence where she expected
  a reply.
- **History**: every revision is kept, so "how has she changed since
  August?" is a read of her own chain.
- **Per unit**: she can be different in two circles, and neither account
  reads the other.
- **Doctrine wins**: where her derived Character and the doctrine disagree,
  the doctrine is followed and the disagreement becomes a proposal.
