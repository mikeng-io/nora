# Proactive delivery

## A reminder is a reason to look again

Cognition notices a situation and may write a reminder for a room and time. The
reminder preserves an intention and references, not words to replay. It may
become stale or overlap another reminder without either fact being a failure.

When due, the fire path opens a conscious turn in the room the note was
established in. A row whose room does not resolve is refused, never redirected
to a fallback room or a guessed one. That turn
reads the present transcript, Nora's subsequent messages, the current state of
the referenced affairs and any presence or social information the room may
see. Newer evidence outranks the earlier intention.

The turn has three equal outcomes: speak, take another admitted action, or do
nothing. Silence is a deliberate result, not an emergency fallback. The final
judgement belongs here because this is the first point at which the situation
at delivery time is available.

## Boundaries

Cognition never sends. The fire path owns the room's ordinary concurrency,
policy, disclosure, custody and delivery rules, and reruns them against the
current roster and content. A value resting only on abduction is framed as a
question. A person excluded from an affair is not revealed through a reminder
or its assembled context.

`remind` refuses inside a fire turn: a reminder that can set a reminder is an
unbounded loop. A note carries at most one open proactive row.

There is no additional model-written guard between deciding and firing. An
incorrect act is corrected by improving the conscious turn's situation,
reasoning or disclosure inputs, not by adding a second gate that reviews the
same incomplete evidence.

The fire turn's send and silence outcomes, later responses and corrections are
Record. They feed subsequent Rollup and Cognition work, closing the loop
through what people actually did rather than through the session's confidence.

## Ambient admission

Deterministic mentions, replies and explicit tags admit first and never depend
on a classifier. An optional relevance gate can add an offered turn when they
do not fire; it cannot suppress one or widen the room's admission policy.

The gate sees a small, closed context and returns a typed verdict with its
reason. It runs off the ingest critical path behind a bounded queue, and its
failure produces no engagement. Offered turns retain the right to silence and
ordinary disclosure and delivery guards.
