# The Cognition session

## One situation, one agent

A session begins with code-built situation and agenda. The situation identifies
what changed, its conversations, authors and ages, relevant member and Notebook
indices, open affairs, recent acts and the clock. Each agenda item states what
happened and binds the room used for disclosure and any resulting act. It never
prescribes the conclusion or a menu of actions.

Pushed context is a small, self-describing index. The session can pull every
read Nora is permitted to use for that unit: transcripts and recorded items,
Rollups, Notebook entries, memory search, media, social reads and admitted web
or place reads. A failed or unwired read answers unavailable, never empty.
Prefetch is a starting point, not a boundary on investigation.

Orient, decide and act share one model context. The session searches before
creating, connects new evidence to existing entities and may change its
understanding without taking an outward act. Ordinary social events already
recorded by code do not require a Notebook restatement. There is no per-message
quota of saved claims and no separate agent for each step or agenda kind.

## What the session may write

The Register holds the unit's affairs. Notes keep open matters, memos keep
settled ones and events hold dated gatherings. Character describes a person,
room, unit or Nora; culture is Character for a collective. Lessons prescribe
what Nora has learned from outcomes. Standing holds her position toward a room,
unit or person. The memory graph and source-tied readings have their own typed
write contract but follow the same evidence and provenance rules.

Revisions name the state they replace. A stale base is refused rather than
overwritten. Correction adds a revision or retirement; it does not erase the
history that made the previous judgement inspectable.

The session may create and adjust its own reminders and self-wakes. A reminder
contains an intention and subject references, not final prose. A person's own
reminder remains under the conscious path's authority except where a validated
event cancellation necessarily changes it.

## Validation before commit

Every persistent write passes code validation before its store call:

- citations resolve to evidence the session was shown or fetched, and every
  evidence chain ends in the Record or a Rollup derived from it;
- the vocabulary, entity kinds, entry shape and lifecycle transitions are legal
  for the destination;
- reasoning mode and confidence are representable, and an inferred or stale
  observation is not promoted into something directly told;
- links between Notebook entries are references, never substitute evidence;
- revisions, authorship, unit scope, subject identity and disclosure exclusions
  are coherent;
- identifier, coordinate, credential and doctrine protections run before
  storage, with the target room's disclosure checked again before any act;
- session, model, instruction and trace provenance is stamped by code.

A refusal returns a typed reason to the session so it can repair the proposal.
A store failure whose commit state is uncertain is unfinished or unavailable,
not proof that nothing was written. Partial valid parts of a graph proposal may
commit only where the store preserves the distinction and records every refusal.

## Reasoning and authority

Directly told, necessarily deduced, generalised from multiple instances and
best-explanation abduction are different promises. Frequency and routine claims
rest on Rollup aggregates rather than one memorable incident. An abduced value
leaves as a question, never a settled fact.

Nora's own words do not independently ground a revision of her Character or
Lessons. They must be paired with people's responses. Her own statement can
ground only the fact that she said it. Third-party memory can suggest what to
investigate but is not itself citation-grade evidence.

Assessment is outside the writing session. It measures correction by people,
unsupported citations, repeated or unanswered acts, stale understanding and
learning drift. It reports; it does not silently edit the Notebook or convert
its own judgement into proof.

## Bounds and termination

Rounds, tokens and wall time bound the whole session. There is no per-tool quota
hidden in the instruction. Tool declarations and result limits still bound
individual calls, and paid external work retains its own spend controls.

An ordinary return ends the session; a stopped or bounded loop fails closed.
Stimuli not comprehended remain pending. Nothing requires each input to produce
a statement, an act or a separate close ceremony: a later self-wake is how the
session retains a matter that needs time to settle.
