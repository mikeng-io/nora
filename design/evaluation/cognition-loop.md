# Cognition journeys

Cognition is measured as a participant in Nora's continuing loop. A wake can
revise understanding, maintain Notebook state, schedule another look or create
a reminder for a conscious turn. Its result is what those changes enable and
prevent later, not only the graph statements written during one session.

The [subject](subject.md), [measurement](measurement.md) and
[coverage](coverage.md) contracts apply unchanged. This subject adds causal
relationships across wakes, not a separate composition or grading system.

## State carries forward

A journey begins with grounded state and external events. Production ingestion
records those events; production eligibility and admission determine when a
Cognition session runs. The session uses its normal context, tools and reads,
then production completion settles claims, lessons, Standing and pending work.
Failure retains unprocessed input according to the runtime's own rules.

Subsequent turns read what the candidate retained. Newly discovered relations
feed later prefetch; lessons can alter a later response; a later fact can
revise or retire an earlier note. Environmental advancement contributes world
events, not another Nora's intervening conclusions or replies.

Self-wakes are inward eligibility for a later look. Reminders enter the normal
conscious fire path, whose reads, delivery or silence and observed responses
become input to later cognition. They are executed against isolated boundaries,
not removed from the object graph to make the journey safe.

A recorded-wake replay deliberately fixes a session boundary. It can inspect
or measure the session and its downstream effects at that boundary, but forcing
a wake cannot establish scheduling, and importing recorded conscious replies
cannot establish candidate engagement. Those limits remain visible in its claim.

## What the evidence must connect

A cross-wake criterion retains the source and changes across the entire causal
window it judges. Person, unit, room and time remain explicit at every link.
Unavailable retrieval is distinct from finding nothing; a bounded partial read
is distinct from a failed read. A link's existence does not establish that a
walk can reach it within its budget and scope.

Persistent effects are checked across every owned state kind, including
revisions, retirement and cancellation. A current view can serve the model but
cannot replace the revision evidence needed to measure changes. Loop evidence
connects discoveries, repeated reads, new links and their later use; an
unrecorded relationship is unknown rather than a zero count.

Each criterion is grounded in the product's intended behaviour. Nothing need
be written merely because a wake ran; a grounded fact need not become a
particular storage kind unless the product requires it. A passed date,
unconfirmed plan or unrelated photo cannot establish that an event completed.

The production loop and its responsibilities are owned by
[Cognition and the Dreamer runtime](../cognition-loop/README.md).
