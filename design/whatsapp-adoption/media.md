# Media and native observations

## Custody before interpretation

Admitted attachments download through the bridge's media boundary into the
custody spool before their observation is journaled. The envelope carries
references, metadata and explicit availability, not an implicit promise that
bytes or a later description exist. Raw contact cards and unnecessary native
payloads are not retained as a shadow media archive.

Voice notes use the same local transcription and media-reading lane as other
platforms. Their native voice-note flag can admit an engagement occasion even
when transcription later fails, but the reply must still say that the content
is unavailable rather than answering an imagined transcript. This differs from
retaining a voice note as a media artifact for later inspection.

Outbound attachments preserve their actual media kind, filename, MIME type and
caption semantics. Audio is not claimed to be a native voice bubble unless the
bridge really sent that form. Every confirmed native attachment handle joins
the same outbound audit row and later reaction or edit resolution.

## Native evidence

WhatsApp reactions, edits, withdrawals, polls, contacts, scheduled events,
pins, membership and room metadata enter the shared typed observation
contract. They do not become fake utterances when no text accompanies them.
A text-bearing edit or service message retains both the delivered utterance
and the independently addressed target.

Poll definitions retain the secret material needed to interpret later votes
within room and creator scope. A vote without that material is unavailable,
not a guessed option or zero tally. A complete empty selection withdraws one
voter's answer; omitted selections and aggregate counts remain partial
evidence with no invented identities.

Contact cards are parsed into bounded intentional fields, with malformed or
unreadable cards marked as coverage gaps. Scheduled-event edits replace the
same artifact, and each responder's answer is independent: not-going is an
answer, an unreadable response is not a withdrawal, and no response count
proves attendance.

Room and membership snapshots replace only their covered scope. An omitted
member is unknown, not removed. Native clocks are retained per field where
they exist; arrival order remains visible where the source supplies no global
ordering.

## Failure and observation

Media download failure, missing custody, unavailable transcription, missing
perception and unsupported native payloads remain different limitations. A
preview or caption is not full content, and a stored metadata field is not a
completed media read.

Source events and queued work preserve their correlation references without
putting raw payloads in telemetry. Counters cover capture, custody, journal,
queue and action outcomes and their costs. The model sees only scoped,
model-safe projections through the ordinary context and tool owners.
