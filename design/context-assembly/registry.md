# Registry, builders and rendering

## Declaration as the review boundary

An occasion's `_BLOCKS` table declares each block's name, order and scope. The
assembler walks it. This makes the occasion's input readable and ensures a new
block is a deliberate row, not an accidental side effect of another feature.

The registry does not give every occasion the same context. A gate can remain
closed to everything except the few blocks its verdict requires; a test holds
that closure even when a dormant extra row would render nothing. Widening is a
change to the table with its own evidence, not a convenience of shared code.

A builder is registered against a meaning, not merely a tag spelling. Two
renderers that give one field different meanings are two block types. Shared
builders preserve one reading of a store while each occasion restamps its own
order and scope.

## Block types and composition

A block type describes what the block is and how to read its fields. This is
where citation grammar, subject identity, age and state are explained; an
objective does not redefine them.

Bodies cross a typed composition boundary. A semantic owner composes its body,
while callers supply no raw text or composer label. Forwarding and rendering
preserve that value until the final renderer explicitly reads its text. This
keeps source flow and responsibility visible without judging the content's
quality.

Empty is a declared outcome, not an omitted block. A declared block renders
with content, self-closes when its store is empty, or reports unavailable.
The occasion decides which states are meaningful; a silent omission is never
allowed to masquerade as an empty world.

## Escaping and identity

Member and stranger text enters through shared renderers and escaping. Speaker
labels cannot forge another row, markup cannot close a block, and a quoted
parent resolves through one timestamp mapping. These are prompt-integrity
rules, not presentation preferences.

Blocks name their subjects and authors by canonical id beside any display name.
That lets a model resolve who a statement concerns without treating a nickname
as an account identifier. Internal ids remain out of prose unless the block's
citation contract explicitly requires them.

Authored framing lives in typed builders. Every value enters escaped, carried,
joined or system-provided paths; there is no bare interpolation. An authored
head that is not a context block stays outside block assembly rather than
inventing a tag for it.

## Occasion-specific contracts

The relevance gate's input stays small and closed because its decision is
admission, not a rich reply. Its escaping and closure are tested directly;
shared assembly does not imply shared inventory.

Cognition and the speaking turns reuse builders where their meanings match,
but they do not inherit one another's purposes. A reply turn needs the current
and quoted message; Cognition needs the agenda and related unit state. A new
occasion declares what its decisions require instead of copying either list.

## Failure and evidence

Unknown blocks, invalid composition and malformed output are errors, never a
successful smaller prompt. Refactoring an existing occasion begins with
rendered golden evidence; byte-identical preservation and a deliberate prompt
change are not the same decision.

The architecture gate finds model calls and requires declaration and assembly.
Its limits are covered by focused tests and review for hand-built text inside
runners, unfamiliar model doors and indirect calls, not by a debt ledger of
permitted exceptions.
