# Composition and exposure

## Filter at render, never at the source

Composition happens after full context assembly and before prefill. It selects
which already-built blocks reach the model; it does not null fields in the typed
context, reparse the message or change tool admission. Resolver inputs and other
consumers see the same frozen context whether or not a block is exposed.

Exposure flags must be passed to consumers that depend on them. A tool cannot be
told that context is already present when the composer withheld it, and it must
remain able to retrieve that context explicitly.

The default is the full current prompt. Invalid names, changed transcript
semantics, malformed answers, timeout and provider failure preserve that full
prompt. A successful reply under fallback is not evidence that the composer
succeeded.

## Floor and selectable units

The current and quoted messages, the reply header, the identity seed and hard
privacy and safety law are fixed. Tool admission, roster and presence facts are
not relevance choices. Transcript extent cannot change without preserving quote
resolution and every positional reference across prompt and tools.

Advisory situation blocks are candidates for selection: documents, delegated
work, optional history and other context whose absence costs an affordance
rather than truth. The identity seed remains even when grown Character would
otherwise supply continuity; the seed is what later evidence was produced
under.

Doctrine separates authored law and identity from learned practice and
instrument knowledge. Law does not become a per-turn relevance option. Practice
belongs to Lessons, grown character to profiles and operating knowledge to the
instrument or runtime skill, with any must-hold boundary kept in law.

## Learned composition is optional

A decision model may judge which optional units a turn needs from the current
message, occasion, recent conversation and short unit cards. New units carry
cards so the inventory can grow without requiring a new output head per feature.
Code owns dependencies, thresholds, the floor and application of the result;
the model never writes the reply or chooses engagement.

Deterministic inclusion is the zero-call baseline. A learned composer must show
supported downstream benefit after its own latency, input and cache cost. A
smaller first call is not sufficient: required-context recall and subsequent
reply quality take precedence, and uncertain selection includes context.

Selective retrieval is a different and larger change. It inserts a decision
before currently concurrent reads and can increase total latency even when it
saves tokens. It is not implied by exposure filtering and requires its own
measured benefit.

## Personal records stay out of data export

Nora answers from records about people rather than exporting those records in
any form, including to their subject. Interpretation and summary are permitted;
row-shaped itineraries, transcript dumps and generated data files are another
form of the same disclosure.

Generated documents, music, fetched public media and artifacts she creates are
not personal-record exports. Existing admission, disclosure, identifier and
coordinate guards remain mechanical boundaries where they apply.
