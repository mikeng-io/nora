# The Two Layers Above The Code

Read in the order doctrine is assembled: `identity` (order 10), `silence`
(25), `conduct` (30). Authority runs downward, so a lower file cannot win an
argument with a higher one, and code cannot win an argument with any of them.

## 1. identity.md — «then you're done» (operator-only)

> You don't linger, and you don't perform either way. You give them the thing
> they actually needed, **then you're done**.

Read whole, the paragraph is about not performing — do not pad, do not linger
for approval — and that reading should survive. But the clause states a
**self-image**, and the self-image is transactional: a turn is a unit of work
that opens on being addressed and closes on being answered.

That is precisely the shape §1 of `00` denies. It is also the doctrine-level
cause of `01 §4.1`: she answered "expect a queue", the turn closed, and the
thread was nobody's. Not a bug — she did what she is.

**What it becomes.** Not a longer paragraph and not a licence to linger; one
clause, changing what she is between turns.

> You give them the thing they actually needed, then you stop talking. Stopping
> talking is not the same as being finished: the things this group has going on
> stay yours to keep track of, and you are the only one here who does.

The two halves of the old sentence are preserved — do not perform, do not
linger in the room — and the third thing, that the *thread* ends with the
message, is removed.

**DEC-163. Operator-only. This gates the bundle** (`00 §7`). Every change in
`03`–`06` is a change the doctrine above it will overrule at runtime while this
sentence stands; `01 §1` is the measurement of exactly that.

## 2. silence.md — the frequency prior

`silence.md` was rewritten on 2026-09-10 (v2 → v3) to name three occasions
instead of two. Two problems survive that rewrite, and one was introduced by it.

### 2.1 «Most turns nobody asked for end that way»

> On the last two, silence is a complete answer — a success, not a failure.
> **Most** turns nobody asked for end that way.

The first sentence is right and stays. The second is a **claim about frequency
presented as guidance**, and it is repeated one layer down in the proactive
objective ("Most mornings there is nothing"). A model reads a stated base rate
as a target.

This is not theory: `01 §3` records d02 collapsing from 5/5 to 1/5 under an
added constraint. Given any reason to hesitate, she resolves toward silence.

**What it becomes.** Keep the permission, drop the quota:

> On the last two, silence is a complete answer — a success, not a failure.
> Say nothing because there is nothing to add, never because saying nothing is
> the safer answer. Being unnecessary once costs a sentence; missing the moment
> costs the thing itself.

That last clause is `00 §5` — the asymmetry, written where she reads it first,
because it is the one piece of judgement she cannot derive from the room.

### 2.2 «You do not speak again into the space after your own reply»

> Whichever way the turn came, it ends when you have said your piece. **You do
> not speak again into the space after your own reply to see how it landed.**
> Coming back later to a thing you decided to come back to is not that.

The rule is right — do not hover after your own message. The carve-out is too
narrow: it licenses only a **scheduled** return. It does not license the thing
`01 §4.1` needs, which is noticing that *her own earlier answer went nowhere*.

**What it becomes.** Add the second carve-out:

> ... Coming back later to a thing you decided to come back to is not that, and
> neither is noticing that something you said days ago was never picked up.

### 2.3 The `not_here` block still points at the old note tier

`silence.md`'s frontmatter says what the file does not cover. It will need the
line added by `04 §4`: which exit a turn takes is not decided here.

## 3. conduct.md — three passages, one of which is the whole boundary

`conduct.md` is `applies_to: [tag, heartbeat, participation, proactive]`. Three
passages written for the tag path are served, unchanged, to a turn whose entire
purpose contradicts them.

### 3.1 «唔好因為上一 turn 講過就翻炒»

> 完咗就畀佢完，唔好因為上一 turn 講過就翻炒。

Correct for a tag turn: do not rehash inside a conversation. Served to
`proactive`, it forbids the feature — coming back to an earlier thread is what
a proactive turn *is*. Both `01 §4.1` and `01 §4.2` are, literally read,
翻炒.

**What it becomes.** Scope it to the conversation rather than to time:

> 呢一 turn 傾完咗嘅，畀佢完，唔好即刻翻炒。但一件冇人跟落去嘅事唔算完 —— 隔咗幾日
> 返嚟接返條線，同即刻重複自己，係兩件事。

### 3.2 «Different things take different records» — the boundary, in doctrine

> Different things take different records: **a settled fact about the person in
> front of you is a memory to keep; something still undecided is an open
> question to track; a thing with a time attached is a reminder.**

**This is the Memory / Note / Reminder boundary, written as doctrine.** It is
the same boundary `03 §2` changes, and it is the doctrine-level reason
`01 §4.4` has nowhere to go: a sprained ankle is a settled fact about a person,
so this rule routes it to memory — and memory never expires, so she will know
about it forever and be prompted about it never.

**What it becomes** (the wording follows `03 §2`, and must not land before it):

> 三種記法，分別喺「幾時唔再啱」：一世都真嘅（佢有兩隻貓）係 memory；而家真、
> 過一排就唔再真嘅（佢隻腳整親咗、我哋打緊呢隻 game）係 note；有一個時刻要出現嘅
> 係 reminder。唔肯定嘅嘢係 note 嘅其中一種，唔係 note 嘅定義。

### 3.3 «未有結論嘅嘢都要記»

> 未有結論嘅嘢都要記：乜嘢仲未定，同埋乜嘢會定到佢。

This is `uncertainty` + `closing_condition` — the note tier's current shape —
expressed as doctrine. It is not wrong; it is **partial**, and it reads as a
definition. Under `03 §1` it becomes one of two shapes.

### 3.4 Not a conflict, but check it after `03`

> Do not re-check what you know or do extra looking for show; a weather
> question needs the weather, not four searches.

Right for the tag path, and in tension with a decide turn that searches the web
for something nobody asked about (`01 §4.3`). The tension is real but weak —
this passage is about *redundant* looking, and a recommendation search is not
redundant. **No change proposed.** Recorded so a later reader does not
rediscover it as a defect.

## 4. Where the asymmetry lives

`00 §5` — early is cheap, late is fatal — could go in three places. It belongs
in `silence.md` (§2.1 above), for one reason: it is a rule about **whether to
speak at all**, and `silence.md` is the file that owns that question and is
read on all three unprompted occasions. Putting it in the proactive objective
would leave the participation exit governed by the old prior; putting it in
`identity.md` would overload a file that should say what she is, not how she
decides.

## 5. Summary of doctrine changes

| file | policy | change | gated on |
|---|---|---|---|
| `identity.md` | **operator-only** | «then you're done» → she keeps track between turns | **DEC-163 — gates all** |
| `silence.md` | agent-may-edit | drop the frequency quota; state the asymmetry | DEC-163 |
| `silence.md` | agent-may-edit | licence to pick up her own dropped answer | DEC-163 |
| `conduct.md` | agent-may-edit | 翻炒 scoped to the conversation, not to time | DEC-163 |
| `conduct.md` | agent-may-edit | the three-way record boundary re-cut on expiry | **DEC-165 (`03 §2`)** |
| `conduct.md` | agent-may-edit | 未有結論 becomes one shape of note, not the definition | DEC-165 |

Note that four of the six are `agent-may-edit` and none of them is a typo fix.
`agent-may-edit` governs *who may write the file*, not *how large a change is
appropriate without asking* — a distinction worth stating, since these six
change what she is for.
