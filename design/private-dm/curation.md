# Curation and publication

## One writer, restricted evidence

The existing unit Cognition and profile writers remain canonical. A private
stimulus can wake a restricted curation session for one owner and anchor,
giving it the bound raw DM evidence and permitted shared context. It does not
pool every member's private transcripts or create a second memory tier.

That session may propose curated writes but cannot send to a group, create
shared reminders, export private artifacts, invoke computer work or use an
asynchronous callback to express raw private material. Failed or held review
leaves evidence pending and creates no shared agenda or draft that bypasses
publication.

Group-facing turns and ordinary Cognition receive only the committed curated
view. They never inherit the curation session's raw transcript, tool cache,
private rationale or working state. Raw DM input is never fed to an uncurated
legacy memory surface or backfill.

## Publication contract

Every DM-derived shared add, revision, retirement, verdict, aggregate or file
write passes a publication check before its native store commits. The check
covers the entire visible payload: title, body, conditions, predictions,
attribution, relationships, aggregate values, filename and metadata. A safe
sentence cannot sanitize a revealing implication elsewhere in the payload.

Explicit keep-private instructions veto the information and conclusions that
would reveal it. Ambiguous confidentiality holds. The judgement considers
source identity, the existence of the private interaction, re-identification
in the destination and whether curation changed the fact's meaning.

The native mutation and its append-only publication receipt commit atomically.
The receipt binds the exact visible-content digest, source scope, target
revision, judgement and writer provenance. Caller-supplied curated flags, a
stale receipt or a citation alone do not authorize the write. Hold, refusal,
error or receipt failure commits neither target nor receipt.

Private provenance remains internally re-derivable but is not included in
shared DTOs, prompt blocks, graph neighbours, portal payloads or explanation
tools. Reading a published target grants no access to its private citations,
source text, session log or rationale.

Curation is a model judgement, not a deterministic privacy theorem. Acceptance
requires useful safe publication as well as refusal of direct, indirect and
aggregate disclosure, independently judged on actual committed views and
actions. A universal hold is not success.

## The laundering rule

A publication into unit X may cite only evidence from X's own rooms and the
DM owner's own words in that DM. It may not cite another unit's messages,
Standing, notes, turn records, Nora replies or tool results derived from that
other unit.

Every DM reply and tool result therefore carries code-computed transitive
lineage from all inputs used. A reply built from another unit inherits that
unit even when its words look like ordinary DM text. Nested and mixed inputs
retain the union. Missing or unresolved lineage refuses publication rather
than passing as the person's own words.

The person's own words are the deliberate exception: they are that person's
disclosure and still undergo the ordinary confidentiality judgement. A
restricted curation session may read other-unit-derived DM items marked with
their lineage, but its validator refuses to cite them into the anchor.

A subject's immediate-hide or forget request is a safety withdrawal, not a
publication. It hides the target without sharing the request or replacement
reason; later reweighing and replacement use the same curation boundary.
