# Understanding and revision

## One interpretation in the wake session

Cognition receives structural anchors, resolution candidates, related profiles,
open affairs and source bodies before the model runs. It can pull further
scoped reads. The [Cognition session](../cognition-loop/session.md) owns the
wake, agenda and outcome; unified memory owns what understanding it may write.

The session asks what changed, what it connects to, whether existing
understanding should move and whether anything deserves an act or a later look.
Understanding may change without a message, reminder, task or lesson. Raw chat
mechanics already recorded by code need no second statement, and no per-message
extraction worker interprets the same input again.

Resolution precedes creation. Code supplies same-scope candidates; the session
matches, creates, relates, corrects or enriches. An unresolved person stays a
reading rather than becoming a model-authored identity. An association selected
by the session is persisted with its Register row and retried against that same
row if linking fails. Proposals do not unlock acts: the session may act on what
it was shown without first saving a statement, and an act's own checks still
apply.

## A vocabulary for what may be written

Events hold plans, occurrences and participation. Places, works and organisations
hold public or source-supported semantic connections. Topics hold recurring
interests. Person relations hold preferences, affiliations and other supported
semantic claims. Readings preserve source-backed statements that no reviewed
relation can represent without distortion.

Profiles hold synthesis, notes hold prospective affairs, lessons hold procedural
learning and decisions hold Nora's working reasoning. Each kind has a create
condition, evidence rule, writer and correction path in one code-owned table.
The model sees the same vocabulary admission enforces. Silence is correct when
no kind fits; an open-affair rule must not be applied to every source item.

A source reading preserves who or what the statement concerns, what was said,
its speech act and its source time. Asked, planned, reported and directly told
remain different. Relative dates resolve against the evidence instant or remain
unknown. A later build never turns an old intention into a current fact.

## Historical understanding

Backfill uses the same session in source-time order with act verbs and
current-profile access withheld — evidence reads and memory proposals only.
Reads and relative-time resolution use the evidence instant, excluding later
knowledge that was not available then. History creates understanding, not
present-day reminders, sends or obligations. A later catch-up considers only
work still open under the real clock.

A replayed session that ended normally records one completion receipt naming
the exact inputs it observed; resume verifies their identities and hashes
rather than trusting that statements were written. A failed or bounded replay
writes no receipt and keeps its own failure accounting, never taking a live
slot. Memory itself updates at wake time, so a strong cue can wake a unit
quickly and a quiet room is revisited after settling.

Edits, late media and new evidence reconsider affected inputs rather than
requiring an indiscriminate replay. Superseded readings remain provenance, not
a second live truth.

## Revision from experience

New observations, explicit corrections and outcomes first identify affected
held understanding. The session reads the eligible sources and current state,
distinguishes changed circumstances from an unsupported earlier interpretation,
and revises only what the evidence warrants. A later turn must then retrieve
the revision under its current scope for the loop to be useful.

A changed fact may need only a relation revision; a recurring pattern may
change a profile; an affair may close; a supported reusable practice may become
a lesson. Feedback never mandates all four. Read the held state before creating
another entry about the same matter.

Nora's intentions, tool attempts, confirmed execution and people's responses
remain separate. Explicit feedback links to the act it concerns; arbitrary
conversation is not promoted into feedback. An unavailable outcome is unknown,
not silence, refusal or success inferred from missing data.

Prior Notebook understanding can guide reads and supply premises but adds no
independent support when repeated. Agreement and engagement measure reception,
not correctness. Learning must not optimize flattery over grounded
understanding, and a narrative about why an error happened is itself an
uncertain judgement.

## Evidence of learning

Persistence, later availability and supported usefulness are different claims.
A write proves only persistence; context injection proves availability, not
use; a later good result alone does not prove the revision caused it.

Evaluation inspects the original sources, held state, proposed revision, actual
later input and action, and outcome. Complete private trajectories support
diagnosis, while public results carry counts and opaque references. Missing,
truncated or unmatched evidence is an error, never a semantic pass. Independent
judges assess support and later use; the writer does not grade its own learning.
